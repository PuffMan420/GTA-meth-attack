The pack is free (name your own price, including $0). The author says
credit is not required. This note is here anyway.

Only the small realistic sheets are included. Each sheet is 512 by 192
pixels: 4 columns and 3 rows of 128 by 64 tiles. Tiles are counted from
the left, top to bottom. Slot 4 on every sheet is empty, so it is not
used. No toon sheets and no large sheets are included.

Files in this folder:
- Road_Asphalt_03-128x64.png
- Road_Asphalt_04-128x64.png
- Road_Asphalt_10-128x64.png
- Road_Asphalt_14-128x64.png
- Road_Asphalt_16-128x64.png

The flat magenta backdrop (red 255, green 0, blue 255) was made
transparent so the tiles can sit on the city. The pictures are
otherwise unchanged.
