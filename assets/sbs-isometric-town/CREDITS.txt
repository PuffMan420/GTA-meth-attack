Files in this folder:
- buildings-1.png, buildings-2.png, buildings-3.png
  The three building sheets. Each tile is 64 by 96 pixels.
  18 columns and 8 rows, 144 tiles per sheet.
- roofs.png
  The roof sheet. Each tile is 144 by 92 pixels.
  3 columns and 4 rows. The last cell is empty, so there are 11 roofs.

The flat teal backdrop (red 0, green 128, blue 128) was made transparent
so the tiles can sit on the city. The pictures are otherwise unchanged.
